Pathology Tissue Release Guide

How to request stored post-surgical wax tissue curls (FFPE) for transcriptomic profiling — with zero new procedures.

Step 1 • Already Stored
No New Surgery or Needles

Every surgery preserves leftover tumor tissue in a small wax block (FFPE) inside your hospital's pathology archive.

Step 2 • Standard Release
Request Thin Wax Curls

Sign your hospital's standard pathology release form asking for 5–10 unstained curls from your existing block.

Step 3 • Team Discussion
Review the 4-Gate Report

Explore how your profile aligns with the 4-Gate Arbiter alongside your treating oncologist at crispro.org.

Technical Instructions for Hospital Pathology Send-Out Window

What to ask the Pathology Department to prepare from the existing archival block:
  • Specimen Type: Archival Formalin-Fixed Paraffin-Embedded (FFPE) surgical resection block (post-chemotherapy interval debulking or primary resection).
  • Sectioning Request: 5 to 10 unstained FFPE curls cut at 10 microns (10 μm) thickness placed into sterile RNase-free Eppendorf tubes (plus 1 H&E reference slide if required by institution).
  • RNA Assay Specification: Total RNA extraction with ribosomal RNA depletion (TruSeq Stranded Total RNA Gold / Ribo-Zero, 40M+ paired-end reads). Note: Do not use poly-A selection on FFPE tissue.
  • Accompanying Document: De-identified surgical pathology report showing block/accession ID and estimated tumor cellularity.
