# CrisPRO SEO Keyword Strategy

This document outlines the keyword strategy for CrisPRO based on SEO best practices. The goal is to attract high-value organic traffic by targeting keywords across the user journey. The methodology is adapted from industry-standard practices, such as those outlined by Ahrefs.

## 1. Core Transactional Keywords

These are high-intent keywords that directly describe our services. They are the primary targets for our main landing pages (Homepage, Co-Pilot App, Investment Thesis).

- **Primary Target**:
  - `AI metastasis prevention` - Our core differentiator.
- **Secondary Targets**:
  - `predictive oncology platform`
  - `AI cancer genomics analysis`
  - `VUS resolution service` - Solves a key pain point.
  - `in silico therapeutic design` - For Pharma/Biotech audience.
  - `AI clinical trial optimization`
  - `oncology co-pilot` - Brand-aligned term.

## 2. Informational & Long-Tail Keywords

These keywords are for blog posts and educational content. The goal is to build authority, educate the market, and attract users in the research phase.

### For Oncologists & Researchers (Problem-focused):
- *How to predict cancer metastasis?*
- *What is a variant of unknown significance?*
- *Challenges in clinical trial recruitment*
- *Interpreting ambiguous genetic test results*
- *Role of AI in oncology treatment planning*

### For Pharma & Biotech (Solution-focused):
- *Using digital twins in cancer research*
- *De-risking preclinical drug development*
- *Automating gRNA design for CRISPR*

### For DeSci & Investors (Innovation-focused):
- *What are IP-NFTs in biotech?*
- *Decentralized science funding models*
- *Future of biotech R&D funding*

## 3. Competitor-Based Keywords

This strategy captures users actively comparing solutions. This can be executed via dedicated comparison pages or in-depth blog posts.

- `Tempus vs CrisPRO`
- `Foundation Medicine alternatives`
- `Guardant Health vs Caris Life Sciences`
- `Best AI oncology platforms`
- `VUS analysis tools comparison`

## Implementation Plan

1.  **On-Page SEO**:
    -   Ensure Core Keywords are used in the `<h1>`, page titles, meta descriptions, and URLs of the main service pages.
    -   The `layout.tsx` metadata has been updated with the primary keywords.
2.  **Content Creation**:
    -   Develop a content calendar based on the "Informational & Long-Tail Keywords." Each keyword represents a potential blog post.
    -   Create detailed comparison pages or articles targeting the "Competitor-Based Keywords."
3.  **Internal Linking**:
    -   When creating new blog content, link back to the core service pages using the Core Transactional Keywords as anchor text. For example, a blog post about VUS should link to the main Co-Pilot page with anchor text like "our VUS resolution service."
