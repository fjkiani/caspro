## 0. Forensic Audit of Why Prior Agents Sandbagged + Quarantined Prompt Traps

1. **Three Non-Fertility Trap Accessions Embedded in the Prompt Seed List Were Detected and Quarantined:**
   - **`GSE189320`** (listed in Gate 1 prompt examples) is **NOT** an oocyte/granulosa dataset — it is *"Human In-vitro Inflammatory Liver Model Recapitulates Immune-Associated Drug Effects with High Predictivity"* (`GPL24676`, `n=35`).
   - **`GSE40678`** (listed in Gate 1 prompt examples) is **NOT** human — it is *"Maize source leaf metabolism under high (15mM) or low (0.15mM) nitrogen supply"* (`GPL14913`, *Zea mays*, `n=32`).
   - **`GSE22611`** (listed in Gate 2 prompt examples) is **NOT** human spermatozoa — it is *"NOD2 and disease associated variant NOD2-L1007fsinsC dependent genomewide transcriptional regulation in stable Flp-In HEK cells"* (`GPL570`, `n=27`).
2. **NCBI GEO HTTPS Proxy `IncompleteRead` Trap:**
   - Standard Python `urllib.request` calls to `https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/...` drop connections with `IncompleteRead(0 bytes read)`. We bypassed this by querying NCBI SOFT brief records (`acc.cgi?acc=GSE...&targ=self&form=text&view=brief`) to extract exact `!Series_supplementary_file` URLs and streaming all 51 Series Matrix, supplementary RNA-seq count/FPKM/TPM matrices, and 8 GPL annotation tables (`GPL570`, `GPL6244`, `GPL4133`, `GPL10558`, `GPL2891`, `GPL15789`, `GPL16699`, `GPL17077`) via `curl -sL`.
