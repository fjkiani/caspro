I wanted to follow up on my last note and share a bit more context on why we reached out.

Over the last few weeks, we spent time decoding prior MSS CRC immunotherapy programs and comparing where those trials failed because of biology versus where they failed because of patient selection, biomarker architecture, or trial design. That exercise made us more interested in BreAK CRC-001, not less, because it highlighted a few areas where preserving interpretability early can materially change what Phase IIa is able to show.

A few themes stood out for us:

In MSS CRC, broad all-comer enrollment has repeatedly diluted signal, while the strongest directional lessons come from enrichment variables such as liver-met status and other biologically separable subgroups.

In multi-component regimens, timing and backbone heterogeneity matter more than most teams expect; even when immune activation is present, it is often not enough to make the trial interpretable unless the design captures the right covariates and biomarker structure.

For BreAK CRC-001 specifically, the combination of exploratory biomarker collection, optional bevacizumab, and upcoming Phase IIa decisions suggests a real opportunity to put a more rigorous biomarker and design framework in place now, before the window narrows.

That is where we think CrisPRO can be genuinely useful: helping turn early translational and clinical data into a credible enrichment and analysis strategy, rather than waiting until after Phase IIa to retrospectively explain the result.

I have attached a brief that walks through the comparator failure landscape in more detail and identifies four specific design questions that we think are worth discussing before Phase IIa enrollment. It is not a critique of the program — the STC-1010 preclinical data is genuinely differentiated, and the Phase Ia safety and DCR signal is encouraging. The brief is an attempt to be useful at a specific moment when the design decisions are still open.

A few things we would want to understand from your side, if you are open to a conversation:

How liver metastasis status is being handled in the Phase IIa analysis plan — whether it is captured as a stratification variable or pre-specified covariate, and what the expected distribution looks like in the enrolled population.

Whether ctDNA or plasma-based molecular biomarkers are included in the exploratory endpoint panel, and if so, what platform and what response threshold you are working with.

How bevacizumab use is being controlled for in the immune biomarker analysis, given that it is optional in the current design.

And separately — the EACR 2025 data on haptenated epitope detection on primed mDCs is a meaningful mechanistic step. We would be curious whether that antigen characterization work extends to a comparison across the three cell lines, particularly given the BRAF status difference between HT29 and the enrolled patient population.

None of these are blocking questions. They are the kind of things that are straightforward to address now and much harder to address after Phase IIa data is in hand.

Happy to set up a call at whatever level of detail is useful — whether that is a high-level conversation about the Phase IIa design or a more technical discussion of the biomarker framework. The brief is attached as a starting point.
