## Competitive Landscape Summary

### What the comparator set tells us about the 1L MSS mCRC field:

1. **The field has not yet produced a positive randomized trial** in 1L MSS mCRC with immunotherapy. QUILT-2.004 (HR 1.061) failed. GRANITE GO-010 shows a trend (HR 0.73) but is underpowered and press-release-only.

2. **Single-arm Chinese trials** (sintilimab, tislelizumab, cadonilimab) show high ORRs (84%+) but are severely underpowered and not generalizable to Western populations. These are hypothesis-generating.

3. **The ctDNA-low patient selection signal** from GRANITE GO-010 (HR 0.50) is the most compelling biomarker hypothesis in the field. BreAK CRC-001 should consider whether ctDNA monitoring is incorporated in its biomarker strategy.

4. **Personalized vs. off-the-shelf vaccines:** GRANITE's personalized neoantigen approach requires 6–8 weeks of manufacturing per patient. STC-1010's whole-cell approach is off-the-shelf — a major commercial and logistical advantage if efficacy is comparable.

5. **Backbone convergence:** mFOLFOX6 + bev is the dominant backbone across direct comparators (QUILT-2.004, SAR445877). XELOX/CapeOX + bev is used in Chinese trials. BreAK CRC-001's mFOLFOX6 ± bev backbone is well-positioned.

6. **Competitive risk:** SAR445877 (NCT07500298) uses the identical backbone (mFOLFOX6 + bev) in 1L MSS mCRC. If SAR445877 advances to Phase 2/3 with positive results, it becomes a direct competitor. Start date 2026-09-03 — watch closely.
