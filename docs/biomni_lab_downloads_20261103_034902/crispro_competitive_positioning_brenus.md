# SECTION 2 — THE PROBLEM BRENUS HAS RIGHT NOW

## 2.1 The Structural Failure Mode That Has Killed Every Prior MSS CRC IO Trial

Microsatellite-stable colorectal cancer constitutes approximately 95% of all metastatic CRC — roughly 140,000 new metastatic cases per year in the US and EU combined `[VERIFIED — Agent 3]`. It is the single largest unmet need in gastrointestinal oncology. Every major checkpoint inhibitor program has failed in unselected MSS CRC:

| Trial | Regimen | Result | Primary Failure Mode |
|-------|---------|--------|---------------------|
| CheckMate 9X8 | Nivolumab + mFOLFOX6 + bev | HR 0.81, p=0.30 — not met | Wrong patients (unselected) |
| QUILT-2.004 | AdCEA vaccine + atezolizumab | HR 1.061, p=0.91 — terminated | Wrong mechanism + wrong patients |
| GRANITE GO-010 | Neoantigen vaccine | Primary endpoint not met | Wrong patients (ctDNA-low subgroup HR 0.50 — exploratory only) |
| IMblaze370 | Atezolizumab + cobimetinib | HR 1.00 — no benefit | Wrong mechanism |
| MODUL | Atezolizumab + bev | No benefit in MSS arm | Wrong patients |

The pattern is consistent: **unselected MSS CRC enrollment dilutes any real signal into noise.** The responding subgroup — non-liver-metastatic, immune-inflamed, pTMB-high — is real but small. Every trial that enrolled all-comers failed to detect it.

## 2.2 The Three Open Design Decisions That Will Determine BreAK CRC-001's Fate

BreAK CRC-001 (NCT06934538) is a Phase I/IIa trial of STC-1010 + mFOLFOX6 ± bevacizumab in first-line MSS/pMMR mCRC `[VERIFIED — ClinicalTrials.gov]`. The Phase Ia safety data is maturing (n=6, no DLTs, 100% DCR `[PRESS-RELEASE-ONLY — AACR 2026 CT051; n=6 only]`). Phase IIa design decisions must be made in the next three to six months.

Three decisions remain open and will determine whether BreAK CRC-001 can detect a real signal:

**Decision 1 — Patient Selection:** Does Phase IIa enroll all-comers MSS mCRC, or does it pre-specify a biomarker-enriched primary analysis population? The current protocol does not stratify by liver metastasis status, pTMB, or Immunoscore `[VERIFIED — NCT06934538 registry]`. Without stratification, the trial will almost certainly replicate the QUILT-2.004 failure pattern.

**Decision 2 — Biomarker Analysis Plan:** The protocol collects an extensive exploratory biomarker dataset (cytokine profiles, immunophenotyping, PBMC markers, ctDNA, HLA expression, TLS evolution) `[VERIFIED — NCT06934538 registry]`. But there is no pre-specified analysis plan, no pre-specified response thresholds, and no assay platform specification in the public protocol `[NOT FOUND IN PUBLIC SOURCES — Agent 2]`. Without a pre-specified plan, the Phase I biomarker data will be analyzed post-hoc and will not be credible to regulators or sophisticated partners.

**Decision 3 — Combination Timing:** STC-1010 is administered alongside mFOLFOX6 ± bevacizumab. mFOLFOX6 causes a lymphopenic nadir at days 7–10 `[MECHANISTIC HYPOTHESIS — Agent 4]`. If STC-1010 is administered during this window, vaccine-driven T-cell priming will be severely impaired. The optimal vaccination timing window relative to mFOLFOX6 cycles and bevacizumab has not been pre-specified.

**These three decisions are time-sensitive.** Phase IIa enrollment will lock the design. A Phase III redesign is far more expensive and time-consuming than a Phase IIa protocol amendment. The window is now.

## 2.3 What Happens If These Decisions Are Made Without Computational Support

The base case — no computational partner, Phase IIa opens with the current design — has a high probability of producing an uninterpretable result. Not a negative result. An uninterpretable one. The 12-month PFS rate in unselected first-line MSS mCRC on mFOLFOX6 ± bevacizumab is approximately 50–55% `[VERIFIED — Agent 3 historical benchmarks]`. A single-arm trial comparing against this benchmark cannot distinguish vaccine-attributable benefit from SOC effect, cannot identify the responding subgroup, and cannot generate the biomarker hypothesis needed for a Phase III design. The program will have consumed Phase IIa resources and produced a dataset that cannot support a regulatory filing or a partnership conversation.
