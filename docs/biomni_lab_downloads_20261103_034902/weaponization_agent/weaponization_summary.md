## Key Intelligence Findings

### 1. CheckMate 9X8 Is the Definitive Null Comparator
The most important finding for STC-1010 positioning: CheckMate 9X8 tested **nivolumab + mFOLFOX6 + bevacizumab** — the identical backbone to BreAK CRC-001 — in 1L mCRC (95% MSS/pMMR) and **failed to meet its primary PFS endpoint** (HR 0.81, p=0.30). The trial did not expand to Phase 3. This is the strongest possible argument that PD-1 checkpoint blockade alone is insufficient in MSS mCRC, and that a mechanistically distinct approach (STC-1010's immune education) is needed.

The data is real and citable, but must not be overstated.

### 4. SC-001 Cannot Be Closed Without Brenus Internal Data
The HT29 BRAF V600E conflict is scientifically legitimate and cannot be resolved from public sources. The most likely resolution (Hypothesis 1: TAA breadth, not neoantigen targeting) is scientifically plausible but unconfirmed. Brenus must provide explicit rationale.

### 5. BOT/BAL Is the Most Credible Competitive Threat — But Heavily Selected

However, the no-liver-met restriction is a major limitation (liver mets present in ~65% of mCRC patients). STC-1010 does not appear to have this restriction. BATTMAN Phase 3 is the key watch item.

### 6. GRANITE Is Dead — But Its Narrative Lives
